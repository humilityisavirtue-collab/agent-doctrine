# The working cards

These are the **unedited** role cards from the running system. [`../LAWS.md`](../LAWS.md) is the
cleaned-up version; this directory is what the agents actually load.

They are published raw on purpose. The edited version reads better and is missing the thing that
makes the laws stick: the specific, dated, slightly embarrassing incident that earned each one.

**What you will see that is not meant for you:**

- **Internal references** like `insight_negative_control_must_mirror_failure` or
  `feedback_immunity_belief_is_the_vulnerability` — memory cards in a private corpus. They are
  provenance, not links. You cannot follow them.
- **Role names by suit** — Spade forms claims, Club proves them, Diamond builds, Nucleus routes. Rename them "author" and "reviewer" and nothing about the laws changes.
- **Message IDs** (`086bd117`) — internal bus messages, kept because a law that cites its
  source is harder to quietly rewrite later.
- **A first-person operator.** The system is one person's lab.

**What is worth your time even so:**

Each card ends with a worked example — a real failure, traced round by round, with the wrong
reasoning left visible. The verification card's table of four consecutive "clean, all four
fixed" reports (each honest, each still red) is the most useful page here. So is the handoff
card's, which shows the same correct diagnosis failing to transfer three times in prose and
landing instantly as raw numbers.

| card | governs | strongest section |
|---|---|---|
| [club-verification.md](club-verification.md) | what counts as *done* | Law 4, "no green on belief" |
| [bus-handoff.md](bus-handoff.md) | what survives between two agents | Law 6, identity as evidence |
| [spade-investigation.md](spade-investigation.md) | what counts as *known* | Law 1, read before you reason |

A card is not finished when it is agreed with. It is finished when someone brings the falsifier.
